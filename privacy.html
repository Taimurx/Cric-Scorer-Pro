1. Introduction

Welcome to Cric Scorer Pro ("the App", "we", "our", or "us").

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and related web services.

By accessing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may collect the following categories of information:

Personal Information (Optional)

When you choose to sign in using Firebase Authentication (e.g. Google Sign-In) to back up your matches, we may receive:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Profile Picture URL (if provided by Google)
  • Firebase Unique User ID (UID)

Note: We never collect, see, or store your passwords. Authentication is handled securely by Google Firebase Authentication infrastructure.

Cricket Match Information (Stored Locally)

The application stores the following cricket data on your device:

  • Team Names & Squad Rosters
  • Player Names & Jersey Numbers
  • Match Settings (Overs, Wickets, Wide/No-ball penalty rules)
  • Toss Decisions
  • Ball-by-Ball Live Scoring Events
  • Batting, Bowling, Extras, and Fall-of-Wickets Statistics
  • Tournament Leaderboard and Points Table Data

Technical Device Telemetry

For diagnostic and compatibility purposes, the app or web app may process:

  • Device Model & Android Version
  • Screen Resolution & Display Refresh Rate
  • Language Preference
  • App Version (e.g. v2.0.11)

3. How We Use Your Information

We strictly use the collected information to:

  • Deliver seamless ball-by-ball cricket scoring services
  • Instantly save, resume, and restore unfinished matches
  • Calculate batting strike rates, bowling economies, and Net Run Rates (NRR)
  • Generate print-ready PDF match scorecards
  • Synchronize match history across your authorized devices
  • Diagnose and patch crashes or technical bugs
  • Maintain zero-ad, distraction-free performance

4. Firebase Authentication

Our application integrates Google Firebase Authentication to verify identity securely for users who opt into cloud sync.

Authentication data is governed under Google's robust cloud security and compliance protocols. You can use the app offline as a guest without signing in.

5. Google Sheets Integration

If you enable the optional Google Sheets export feature, the app creates a spreadsheet containing the match scorecard within your own authorized Google Drive account. We never access, read, or modify any files outside the specific cricket sheets created by the app.

6. Data Storage & Encryption

All offline matches are saved inside Android's secure app-private SQLite/SharedPreferences storage. For cloud backup, data is transmitted over TLS/HTTPS encryption and stored on secure Firebase Cloud infrastructure.

7. Data Sharing & Sale Policy

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or cricket match data to any third-party advertisers, data brokers, or commercial entities.

Data is only shared when necessary to fulfill your requests (such as generating PDF scorecards or syncing via Firebase) or if required by applicable law.

8. Cookies & Local Storage

On the web version, browser LocalStorage is utilized solely to remember your chosen language (English/Bengali), theme preference (Sunrise, Ocean, or Midnight), and active offline match states.

9. Security Practices

We employ modern industry standards including end-to-end HTTPS transmission, cryptographic authentication tokens, sandboxed device storage, and least-privilege security permissions.

10. Children's Privacy

Cric Scorer Pro is a sports utility application suitable for cricket enthusiasts of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate parental or school consent.

11. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You maintain full ownership of your cricket data. You may at any time:

  • Export all your match scorecards to PDF or spreadsheets
  • Clear all local match history directly from the app settings
  • Request complete account and cloud deletion by contacting our support desk

12. Third-Party Service Providers

The application relies on trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase: Cloud database and secure authentication
  • Google Drive / Sheets API: Optional scorecard spreadsheet exports
  • Vercel: Official download website hosting

13. International Data Transfers

If you use cloud backup, data may be stored on secure Google servers located internationally in accordance with standard contractual clauses and data privacy safeguards.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect new features or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted immediately on this page with an updated effective date.

15. Contact Us & Support Desk

For any privacy inquiries, bug reports, feature suggestions, or support regarding Cric Scorer Pro, please reach out directly:

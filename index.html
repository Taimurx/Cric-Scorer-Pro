FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We have got answers right here.

Is Cric Scorer Pro completely free?
Yes, 100% free with no in-app purchases, no hidden subscriptions, and zero advertisements during live scoring.
Does it require an active internet connection?
No. Live scoring, PDF export, player management, and tournaments work completely offline. Internet is only optionally required if you want to back up match archives to Google Drive/Cloud.
What happens if my phone dies or the app closes mid-match?
Never! Every single delivery, run, and wicket is immediately written to local persistent memory. Reopening the app prompts you to immediately resume your ongoing match from the exact ball you stopped at.
Can I export and share full scorecards via WhatsApp?
Yes! You can export a crisp, print-ready PDF scorecard with detailed batting, bowling, fall of wickets, and extras. It can be shared directly to WhatsApp groups, Facebook, or printed.
Which Android versions are supported?
The app supports Android 7.0 (Nougat), 8.0, 9.0, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14 and above, across all phone brands including Samsung, Xiaomi, Realme, Vivo, OnePlus, and Oppo.
Can I run full tournament leagues with Super Overs?
Yes! You can organize both Knockout and League tournaments with automated standings, Net Run Rate (NRR) computation, and super over tiebreaker handling.
How does Google Sign-In & Cloud Sync work across Web and Android?
Sign in with your Google account to automatically back up your matches, squads, and tournaments to Firebase Cloud. You can score on your Android phone and view or resume matches on your PC or mobile web browser seamlessly.
